The bundled Kuala Lumpur / central Selangor map roads and directed routing
graph are derived from the Geofabrik Malaysia-Singapore-Brunei OpenStreetMap
extract dated 2026-11-21.
OpenStreetMap data is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL)
1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The bundled overview textures are renderings of the same OSM-derived road
database. Source extract: https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/malaysia-singapore-brunei.html
The map and route graph only cover central Kuala Lumpur and central Selangor.
See kl_selangor_roads.metadata.json and kl_selangor_route_graph.metadata.json
for geographic bounds and source details. This is not a live map or a complete
Malaysian road database. City and locality tags are taken from the same source
extract. Route travel speeds by OSM road class are simplified game estimates,
not live traffic or official travel times. Road graph building preserves OSM
one-way/access tags and excludes road classes unsuitable for game partners.
